# The Engineer's Daily
Version: 0.1

## Mission

Cultivate engineers who love God, serve people, and pursue excellence in their craft.

We believe engineering is an opportunity to exercise wisdom, stewardship, integrity, humility, and service through the work of building software that benefits others.

Our goal is not merely to teach engineering, but to cultivate engineers.

---

## Foundation

Jesus Christ is the beginning and the end of this curriculum.

Everything we build should help engineers better understand their vocation as followers of Christ.

---

## Design Constraints

- Simplicity wins.
- Ambiguity is the enemy.
- Formation over information.
- Every lesson teaches one engineering principle.
- Every lesson cultivates one biblical virtue.
- Every lesson contains one story.
- Every lesson has one technical deliverable.
- Every lesson ends with reflection.
- Every lesson naturally leads to the next.
- Every season must define Theme, Duration, Final Artifact, and Formation Outcome. Nothing more.
- Never force Scripture into engineering. Let Scripture shape the engineer.
